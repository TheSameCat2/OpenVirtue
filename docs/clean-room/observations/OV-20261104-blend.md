# OV-20261104 — GHOST and DIAPHANOUS blending; region surface offsets

Status: confirmed (one round, six carriers) and implemented.

## Question
The retail title's head shadows are `DIAPHANOUS, BEHIND, SHADOW` strips of the palette's black
on a `BLUR` palette (21 `DIAPHANOUS` textures in all), and its scripts scroll floors with
`FLOOR_OFFS_X` and `FLOOR_OFFS_Y` at run time. The manual (p.44) says `GHOST` shows a texture
"semi-transparent on things, actors or transparent walls" and `DIAPHANOUS` "like GHOST, except
… diaphanous and not dithered", working only with a `BLUR` palette. What pixels does each
draw, what does `DIAPHANOUS` draw without `BLUR`, and which way do a region's surface offsets
slide its texture?

## Method
- Oracle: native Linux DOSBox-X 2026.10.01 with `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`, `forcerate=60`, the
  SaintsX-patched `VRUN.EXE` V3.963.
- Fixture generator: `ovtool oracle prepare blend <dir>` (`tools/OpenVirtue.Tools/BlendProbeCases.cs`).
  The floor is the gray ramp's first index (16, declared `RANGE 16, 32`; the ramp runs 248 to 0
  in steps of 8), shaded by depth to index 32 (120, 120, 120) at 24 steps. A thing of the
  32×48 orange bitmap (index 11: 240, 130, 40) stands there with a 48×12 strip of index 1 (the
  `INFINITY` black) attached as a `SHADOW`, so the strip's upper half lies over the base and its
  lower half over the floor; the final screenshot's indices in the strip are read against the
  floor's beside it. The offset cases put the texmap probe's 64×64 cell swatch on the floor and
  ceiling (`SCALE` 4: a cell a step) with `FLOOR_OFFS_X 16; FLOOR_OFFS_Y 32` and
  `CEIL_OFFS_X 8; CEIL_OFFS_Y 24`. Traces (`OVBLD0.TXT`, two lines) hash `dce49209`.
- Confirmation that no proprietary code/assets are committed: synthetic level and bitmaps only;
  nothing under `_research/` is committed.

## Readings
| Carrier | Reading |
|---|---|
| control (the strip plain, `FLAGS BLUR`) | the strip index 1 over the base and the floor; the floor beside it index 32 in those rows |
| diaph (`DIAPHANOUS, SHADOW`, `FLAGS BLUR`) | over the floor the strip reads index 9 (60, 60, 60: the ceiling ink, the exact mean of 120 and 0); over the orange base index 39 (64, 64, 64), the ramp entry nearest the mean 120, 65, 20 (entries 38 and 39 tie in 6-bit distance) |
| diaph-noblur (no `BLUR`) | the strip reads index 0 everywhere |
| ghost (`GHOST, SHADOW`, `FLAGS BLUR`) | a checkerboard: the strip's index 1 where the screen pixel's x + y is odd, the floor's 32 or the base's 11 where it is even, in every row of the strip |
| floor-offs | cell column (−x − 4) mod 16 (12 at x 31.8 and 47.1, 15 at 44.7), cell row (y + 8) mod 16 (8 at y 16.9, 9 at 1.1, 6 at −1.1, 0 at −7.9) |
| ceil-offs | cell column (−x − 2) mod 16 (0 at x 29.6, 14 at 47.1), cell row (y + 6) mod 16 (0 at y 10.1, 7 at 1.1, 4 at −1.1) |

- **`GHOST` draws the texture on the screen pixels whose x + y (from the top left) is odd and
  leaves the rest to what is behind**: a fixed checkerboard, whatever the texture.
- **`DIAPHANOUS` with a `BLUR` palette draws the palette colour nearest the mean of the texel's
  colour and the pixel's behind it**; a tie goes either way. **Without a `BLUR` palette it draws
  palette entry 0** (black in the retail palettes).
- **A region's `FLOOR_OFFS_X` or `CEIL_OFFS_X` comes off the texture column and its
  `FLOOR_OFFS_Y` or `CEIL_OFFS_Y` adds to the row**, in texture pixels: column =
  floor(−x · SCALE_X − OFFS_X), row = floor(y · SCALE_Y + OFFS_Y) (OV-20261104-texmap's axes).

## Open Questions
- The nearest-colour metric of the blur table (a tie here between two ramp entries) and
  `DIAPHANOUS` or `GHOST` on transparent walls and wall attaches.
- Whether `DIAPHANOUS` with a `BLUR` palette that is not the current one (the retail title's
  black start palette has none, its level palette has) blends or draws entry 0.

## Implementation
`LevelTexture.IsGhost`/`IsDiaphanous` and `LevelPaletteShading.Blur` are parsed; `SpriteBuilder`
tags each layer with a `SpriteBlend`; the host turns it into a `DrawBatch.Blend` of
`Ghost`, `Diaphanous` (a level with a `BLUR` palette) or `Black` (none); the GL shader discards
the checkerboard's even pixels for `Ghost`, blends half for `Diaphanous` (a mean in RGB rather
than the blur table's nearest colour) and draws black for `Black`. `MeshBuilder.SurfaceVertex`
takes the region's X offset off the column. Tests: `SpriteBuilderTests`
(`Build_GhostAndDiaphanousTextures_CarryTheirBlend`), `RuntimeHostBlendTests`,
`MeshBuilderTests` (`Build_AppliesRegionSurfaceTextureOffsetsInPixels`).
