# OV-20261029-sky: where the original draws a SKY texture
Date: 2026-10-29
Status: confirmed (three rounds; round 1 failed to compile) and implemented
Observer: OpenVirtue maintainers (black-box probe, screenshot measurement)
Implementer: OpenVirtue maintainers

## Question
How does the original map a `FLAGS SKY` texture onto the screen: which side faces which direction,
which way and how fast it scrolls when the player turns, how it scales with `PLAYER_ARC`, where it
sits vertically, how `PLAYER_TILT` moves it, and what `SKY_OFFS_X/Y` do?

## Why It Matters
OpenVirtue stretched one side of the sky over the whole view, chosen by a compass guess.

## Clean-Room Boundary
- Oracle used: DOSBox-X run of the SaintsX-patched DOS runtime (V3.963); `SCREENSHOT` captures
  measured with our own script (palette indices along rows and columns).
- Reference material consulted: the 1998 A3 manual p.36–38 (sky sides over 360°, horizontal scale
  from width and sides, no vertical scaling, lower edge "usually" on the horizon, edge rows repeated),
  p.103 (`SKY_OFFS_X/Y`).
- Split-role mode used: not needed (black-box).
- Local-only artifacts: `_research/oracle-runs/OV-20261029-sky/` (`round2/`, `round3/`).
- Relevant local-only file hashes (sha256, first 8), `OVSKS0.PCX`: round2 angle-0 `1017d519`,
  angle-0.2 `3f3d0658`, angle-1.6 `dcd67bf2`, arc-2 `95a0e81a`, offs-x `a55318db`; round3 high
  `ea54d110`, high-tilt-up `fd6d8e3c`, high-offs-y `bec6886c`.
- Confirmation that no proprietary code/assets are committed: synthetic carriers only.

## Setup
Native Linux DOSBox-X 2026.10.01, S640x480, `forcerate=60`; carriers from `ovtool oracle prepare sky`
(`tools/OpenVirtue.Tools/SkyProbeCases.cs`). The room's ceiling is `sky_tex { SIDES 4; BMAPS a, b,
c, d; FLAGS SKY; }`; each 64 × 64 side has its left half red, green, blue or magenta, its right half
yellow, and a marker band over rows 16–47. Round 1 declared the texture after the room and failed
("Error(137): Texture unknown"); round 3 raises the eye to 15 in the 16-high room so the far wall's top
is just above the eye line and the band shows.

| angle-0.2, 0.4 | the boundary at 265, 393 | +128 px per 0.2 rad: linear, 640 px/rad, the sky moves right as the angle grows |
| angle-1.6 | yellow 0–155, green 156– | side 1 lies to the left of side 0: sides go counter-clockwise |
| arc-2 | green, yellow 138–388, red 389– | 320 px/rad; side 0 starts at 389 |
| offs-x (`SKY_OFFS_X` 16) | the boundary at 153 | 16 window pixels to the right |
| high | band rows 193–224 | 32 rows for 32 bitmap rows; bitmap row 0 at 177, row 63 at 240 (the eye line) |
| high-tilt-up (−0.1), high-tilt-down (0.1) | band at 240–271, 145–176 | the world's tilt shear (±48 rows; up 47) |
| high-offs-y (`SKY_OFFS_Y` 8) | band from 201 | 8 rows down |
| angle-0 column | yellow (side colour fill) above row 177 is invisible behind the far wall at ARC 1; at ARC 2 the band is cut by the wall's top | consistent with the rows above |

The side-0 boundary at both arcs fits x = W + (W/ARC)·(angle − π/4): at angle 0 the right edge of
the window shows the centre of side 0.

## Invariant
Column x (0…W) of the 3-D window shows the direction σ = `PLAYER_ANGLE` + `PLAYER_ARC`·(W − x)/W
(the right edge the view angle, the left edge a whole field of view to the left), shifted
`SKY_OFFS_X` pixels to the right. Side k of N (the `BMAPS` order) is centred on σ = k·2π/N and spans
2π/N, its bitmap's left column at the more counter-clockwise end. Vertically the bitmap is drawn one
window row per row, its last row on the eye line `H/2 − PLAYER_TILT·H`, `SKY_OFFS_Y` rows lower;
above and below it its top and bottom rows repeat.

## Open Questions
- The texture's own `OFFSET_X/Y`, windows narrower than the screen, and low-resolution `VIDEO` modes
  (whether a row is a window row or a virtual pixel there).

## Implementation
`OpenVirtue.Engine.Rendering.SkyPanorama` (`Columns`, `TopRow`) and `RuntimeHost.AddSky`; tests
`SkyPanoramaTests` reproduce the measured boundaries and rows.
