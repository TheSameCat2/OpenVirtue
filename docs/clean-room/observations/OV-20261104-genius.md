# OV-20261104 — GENIUS light on a wall: one value a wall, by the midpoint cosine

Status: confirmed (two rounds, seventeen carriers) and implemented.

## Question
A region's `GENIUS` is a thing or actor whose texture's `AMBIENT` makes it the region's light
source; a wall texture's `ALBEDO` is its reflection of that light (manual p.33, p.37, p.58). The
retail levels use `GENIUS` 13 times and `ALBEDO` on 33 wall textures. OV-20261029-shading rounds
4–5 saw a facing wall gain `ALBEDO` × 16 shade levels and grazing walls gain less, but only on the
far ends of the side walls. How does the gain run along a wall, with the angle, the distance and
the lamp's `AMBIENT`, and what of a lamp behind the wall?

## Method
- Oracle: native Linux DOSBox-X 2026.10.01 with `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`, `forcerate=60`, the
  SaintsX-patched `VRUN.EXE` V3.963.
- Fixture generator: `ovtool oracle prepare genius <dir>` (`tools/OpenVirtue.Tools/GeniusProbeCases.cs`).
  A free wall 16 steps long (map y −8…8 at x 32) stands 32 steps ahead of the player in the
  gray ramp's first colour (`RANGE 16, 32`, bright to black in 32 steps), its texture `ALBEDO` 1
  (0.5 in one case); the room's `AMBIENT` is 0.25 (0.5 in two) and `PLAYER_LIGHT` 0, so the wall
  reads level 22 (14) without the lamp. The room's `GENIUS` is an invisible thing whose texture
  has `AMBIENT` 1 (0.5 in one case), placed at (0, 0), (8, 0), (24, 0), (30, 0), (24, 8),
  (32, 12), (40, 0) in round 1 and (28, 8), (24, 16), (26, 2), (24, 24), (24, 32), (40, 0),
  (40, 8) in round 2. The index along the eye-line row across the wall, 16 plus the shade level,
  is read every 16 pixels. Traces (`OVGEN0.TXT`, two lines) hash `dce49209`.
- Confirmation that no proprietary code/assets are committed: synthetic level only; nothing under
  `_research/` is committed.

## Readings
Every wall reads one level along its whole length. With the lamp at (x, y), the cosine at the
wall's midpoint (32, 0) between the wall's normal toward the player (−x) and the lamp:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| (0, 0), 32 away | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 1 | 6 | 6 |
| (8, 0), 24 away | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 1 | 6 | 6 |
| (24, 0), 8 away | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 1 | 6 | 6 |
| (30, 0), 2 away | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 1 | 6 | 6 |
| (26, 2) | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 0.949 | 6 | 6.82 |
| (24, 8) | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 0.707 | 10 | 10.69 |
| (28, 8) | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 0.447 | 14 | 14.84 |
| (24, 16) | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 0.447 | 14 | 14.84 |
| (24, 24) | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 0.316 | 16 | 16.94 |
| (24, 32) | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 0.243 | 18 | 18.12 |
| (32, 12), on the wall's line | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | 0 | 22 | 22 |
| (24, 0) | 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 | 1 | 14 | 14 |
| (24, 0) | 0.25 / 1 / 0.5 | 1 | 6 | 6 |
| (40, 0), behind | 0.5 / 1 / 1 | −1 | 30 | 30 |
| (40, 8), behind | 0.5 / 1 / 1 | −0.707 | 24 | 25.31 |
| (40, 0), behind | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | −1 | index 1 | 38 |
| (40, 8), behind | 0.25 / 1 / 1 | −0.707 | index 1 | 33.31 |

- **A `GENIUS` lamp gives a wall one shade gain along its whole length: 16 · `ALBEDO` · cos θ
  levels, θ the angle at the wall's midpoint between the wall's normal and the lamp**, whatever
  the distance (2 to 32 steps alike) and whatever the lamp texture's `AMBIENT` (1 or 0.5 alike);
  the level is then rounded down to an even one as any shade level.
- **A lamp behind the wall darkens it by the same rule** (cos θ negative).
- **A level of 32 or more, past the range's end, shows the `INFINITY` colour**, palette entry 1,
  not the range's last entry.

## Open Questions

- A lamp's effect on floors and ceilings, and on sprites.

## Implementation
`MeshBuilder.GeniusLight` returns `ALBEDO` × 0.5 × cos θ of ambient (16 levels a 1.0) from the
wall's midpoint, negative behind the wall; `PaletteShading.InfinityLevel` (32) and the GL shader
show palette entry 1 past the range. Tests: `MeshBuilderTests`
(`BuildWalls_GeniusLamp_LightsTheWallByTheMidpointCosine`), `PaletteShadingTests`.
