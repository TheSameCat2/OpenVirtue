# OV-20261104 — LEVEL: what a level change keeps

Status: confirmed (one carrier) and implemented.

## Question
The retail title changes level with `LEVEL "apathy.wdl", "apathy.wrs"` after a `SAVE_INFO`, and
reads the saved state back with `LOAD_INFO` in the new level's start. The manual (p.88) says
`LEVEL` loads the new world, runs its `IF_START` and replaces every region, object and skill but
the `GLOBAL` ones; p.101 says `GLOBAL` skills are reloaded with `LOAD_INFO` after a level change.
Does the action go on after the instruction, does `IF_START` run again, do `TICKS` and
`TOTAL_FRAMES` start over, which skills survive, and what of a waiting action?

## Method
- Oracle: native Linux DOSBox-X 2026.10.01 with `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`, `forcerate=60`, the
  SaintsX-patched `VRUN.EXE` V3.963, about 0.26 ticks a cycle.
- Fixture generator: `ovtool oracle prepare level <dir>` (`tools/OpenVirtue.Tools/LevelProbeCases.cs`).
  The probe action counts a `GLOBAL` skill (`lv_g`) and a plain one (`lv_p`) once per pass, arms a
  per-cycle sampler counting a plain and a `GLOBAL` skill, and on its first pass beams the player
  to x 5, `CALL`s an action in `WAITT 64` (`W`), waits 8 ticks (`L`), runs `LEVEL "TITLE.WDL"`
  (its own world), prints `AFTER`, waits 8 and prints `AFTER8`; the second pass waits 40 and prints
  `E`. Trace `OVLVL0.TXT` sha256 `024ac989`; `OVSTA0.TXT` holds a `START` per `IF_START`.
- Confirmation that no proprietary code/assets are committed: synthetic level only; nothing under
  `_research/` is committed.

## Readings
| Line | `TICKS` | `TOTAL_FRAMES` | `lv_g` | `lv_p` | `lv_n` | `lv_gn` | `PLAYER_X` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `S` (pass 1) | 13.406 | 51 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `L` | 20.469 | 78 | 1 | 1 | 27 | 27 | 5 |
| `AFTER` | 20.469 | 78 | 1 | 1 | 27 | 27 | 5 |
| `S` (pass 2, after a second `START` and `BEGIN`) | 13.313 | 51 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 28 | 0 |
| `E` | 52.344 | 200 | 2 | 1 | 149 | 177 | 0 |

- **The action goes on past `LEVEL`** (`AFTER` in the same cycle); the `WAITT 8` after it never
  resumed, nor did the `CALL`ed `WAITT 64` (`W`): the new world starts with no waiting action.
- **`IF_START` runs again, with `TICKS` and `TOTAL_FRAMES` started over** (the second pass's `S`
  at 13.313 and 51 after the start-up's `WAITT 16`, as the first's).
- **`GLOBAL` skills keep their values across the change** (`lv_g` 2, the sampler's `lv_gn` 28 and
  counting on); plain skills start over (`lv_p` 1, `lv_n` 0). The player stands at the start
  again (`PLAYER_X` 0, `PLAYER_ANGLE` 0).

## Implementation
`WdlInterpreter`: `LEVEL` no longer ends the action (`RuntimeHost` reloads the world after the
cycle, with `CaptureLevelTransitionGlobals`/`RestoreLevelTransitionGlobals` as before, and the
new runtime starts with no waiting action). The carrier's two passes replay in structure
(`OVSTA0.TXT` matches); the replay's clock cannot anchor a trace whose sampler count starts over.
