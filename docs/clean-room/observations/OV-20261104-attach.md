# OV-20261104 — ATTACH textures on things, actors and walls; the wall texture's rows

Status: confirmed (seven rounds, 27 carriers) and implemented.

## Question
The retail title attaches a wide, low shadow strip (`FLAGS DIAPHANOUS, BEHIND, SHADOW`, an 80×9
bitmap) to its head actors, a cross and cracks to walls with `POS_X`/`POS_Y`, and buttons to
walls. The manual (pp. 42–45) says an `ATTACH` texture appears at the pixel position `POS_X`,
`POS_Y` "relative to the upper left corner of the original texture", `BEHIND` "behind the
original texture", and `SHADOW` "at the actor's floor height"; for walls it gives a rule of thumb
in wall texture pixels. Where exactly is each layer drawn, at whose scale, is a shadow a flat
spot on the floor or an upright strip, what cuts an attach, and which way do a wall texture's
rows and columns run?

## Method

  SaintsX-patched `VRUN.EXE` V3.963.
- Fixture generator: `ovtool oracle prepare attach <dir>` (`tools/OpenVirtue.Tools/AttachProbeCases.cs`).
  A thing of a 32×48 orange bitmap stands 24 steps ahead of the player (eye 8, `PLAYER_ARC` 1:
  24.4 pixels a step across and 27.5 up at that depth, the floor on row 460); its attach is a
  48×12 magenta strip or a 16×16 cyan square. A free wall of the 64 px blue texture (`SCALE` 4)
  8 steps long stands 32 steps ahead (18.3 and 20.6 pixels a step; ceiling row 76, floor 405).
  The final screenshot's palette indices give each layer's rectangle. Rounds 1–3 placed the thing
  16 steps ahead with a yellow bitmap, which the far wall's yellow target swallowed, and the
  floor there lies below the screen; they are superseded. The wall texture's directions come from
  the fade-pal probe's `swatch` carrier (OV-20261104-fade-pal), a 16×16 bitmap of every index on
  a wall at `SCALE` 2. Traces (`OVATT0.TXT`, two lines) hash `dce49209`.
- Confirmation that no proprietary code/assets are committed: synthetic level and bitmaps only;
  nothing under `_research/` is committed.

- **A thing's or actor's sprite is its bitmap's pixels over its texture's `SCALE_X` and
  `SCALE_Y` steps, 16 a step by default**, standing on the floor, centred on its position.
- **An attach is an upright sprite of its own size (its bitmap over its own `SCALE`), its upper
  left corner `POS_X`, `POS_Y` pixels of the base texture, at the base's scale, right of and
  below the base's upper left corner**; it is drawn over the base, or behind it with `BEHIND`,
  and is not cut at the floor.
- **A `SHADOW` attach is the same upright sprite with its vertical centre on the region's floor
  height** (not a spot on the floor), `POS_X` and `POS_Y` shifting it as for any attach.
- **A wall's attach lies on the wall at its own size, its upper left `POS_X` wall texture
  pixels (at the wall texture's `SCALE_X`) along the wall from the texture's u origin at the
  second vertex and `POS_Y` such pixels (at `SCALE_Y`) below the top of the wall texture's first
  repeat above the floor**, cut to the wall. `PORTCULLIS` changes nothing for a wall standing on
  the floor.
- **A wall texture's columns run from the second vertex toward the first, and its rows run
  downward from the top of each repeat, the bitmap's bottom row on the floor.** Repeats are
  stacked from the floor.

## Open Questions
- Whether an attach on a wall taller than one repeat appears once or in every repeat. (The
  repeats stack from height 0 — OV-20261104-texmap — and so does the attach's origin: round 7.)
- A shadow under a thing raised above the floor (the `SET` of a thing's `Z` did not move it);
  `GHOST` and `DIAPHANOUS` blending; a second attach chained to an attach texture; `OFFSET_X`
  and `OFFSET_Y` of a wall texture.
- `PORTCULLIS` and `POSITION` on a wall raised above its floor.
- Whether a plain floor or ceiling ever hides a sprite (a sprite in a pit): OpenVirtue lets none
  hide one, as the probe's floor did not, and keeps a `BELOW` stack's slabs hiding by the storey
  rule.

## Implementation
`SpriteBuilder` places the attach layer from the base layer's upper left corner by `POS_X` and
`POS_Y` over the base's scale, at the attach's own size, in front of or behind the base, a
`SHADOW` attach centred on the region's floor height; `LevelTexture` carries `PosX` and `PosY`.
`MeshBuilder.EmitWallAttachment` lays a wall's attach quad on the wall from the second vertex
and the top of the texture's first repeat above the floor, cut to the wall, a hair off each
face; `EmitWallSegment` now runs the texture's rows downward from the top of each repeat. Floors
and ceilings are their own mesh batches (`MeshBatch.Surface`) that the host draws without writing
depth (`DrawBatch.DepthWrite`), since the original lets a sprite reach below the floor line (pos)
and only walls hide one; the surfaces of a `BELOW` stack's regions keep writing depth, since a
sight line never leaves its storey within the stack's footprint (OV-20261029-below-view).
`AttachFrameTests` draws the pos and wall-pos scenes on the GL host and checks the three
rectangles against the readings above within two pixels. Tests:
`SpriteBuilderTests` (`Build_AttachTexture_SitsAtPosFromTheBasesUpperLeftInTheBasesPixels`,
`Build_ShadowAttachTexture_LiesOnRegionFloor`), `MeshBuilderTests`
(`BuildWalls_AttachTexture_LiesAtItsPosFromTheWallTexturesUpperLeft`,
`BuildWalls_AttachTexture_IsCutToTheWall`, `BuildWalls_UsesTextureScaleAsPixelsPerStep`).
